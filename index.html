How scoring works

Evaluation methodology

EvalForge converts 1–5 ratings into a 100-point weighted score. Unrated dimensions contribute zero and remain visibly incomplete, preventing partial reviews from appearing final.

Weighted scoreΣ (rating ÷ 5 × dimension weight)A result within two points is treated as a tie.
